Welcome

The Heart of the Philippines

The National Capital Region, also known as Metro Manila, is the country’s center of government, business and culture. It is made up of 16 cities and one municipality, including Manila, Quezon City, Makati, Taguig, Pasay and Pasig. Our package brings together its best historic sites, museums, food districts and waterfront views in one easy, well-paced trip.

Good to know

Travel Tips & FAQ

What should I bring?

Comfortable shoes, sunscreen, a hat, an umbrella, a refillable bottle and a valid ID.

What is the best time to visit?

November to February is cooler and drier, ideal for walking tours.

How do I get around on my own?

You can use the LRT/MRT, buses, jeepneys and ride-hailing apps. The package already includes van transfers.

What should I wear?

Light, breathable clothes. Dress modestly when visiting churches.

Is it safe?

Keep your valuables close in crowded areas and stay with your group, as in any big city.
